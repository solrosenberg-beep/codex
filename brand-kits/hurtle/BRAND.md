# Hurtle brand kit (for Amazon listing images)

Source: Hurtle Brand Guide, 2025, by Blank Media (full PDF in this folder). Short version an agent reads before briefing or generating any Hurtle image. Where the brand guide and the Sound Around image masterclass rules disagree on text size, the masterclass wins: everything must read on a phone thumbnail. The brand guide controls colors, fonts, logo, and tone.

## Who buys Hurtle

Parents buying active gear for kids: pogo sticks, climbers, bouncing shoes, scooters, helmets. Brand words: safe, kids, imaginative, modern, purposeful, friendly, whimsical, playful, ownable, energetic. The buyer's worry is "will my kid get hurt" and "will they actually use it." Images should show real kids in motion and answer the safety question in big type (weight limit, age range, grip, padding).

## Colors (use exactly these)

| Name | HEX | RGB | PMS | Role | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Purple | #522C81 | 82, 44, 129 | 3583 C | Primary. Logo and copy. Brand anchor. | 40%, logo and copy only |
| Orange | #FE9141 | 254, 145, 65 | 6017 C | Backgrounds and buttons. Energy and contrast. | 35% backgrounds, 5% CTAs |
| Light Gray | #F1F1ED | 241, 241, 237 | Cool Gray 1 C | Backgrounds only. Makes it feel premium and modern. | 20% |
| White | #FFFFFF | 255, 255, 255 | | Copy on dark backgrounds. Not a core brand color. | 20% |

Approved text/background pairs:
- White on Orange
- White on Purple
- Purple on Light Gray
- Purple on White
- Orange headline accent on Purple (used in the guide's product tiles)

Never: Purple logo or copy on Orange, Orange logo, Gray logo, gradients, any color outside these four.

## Fonts

| Role | Font | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headline | Cocogoose Pro | Darkmode | Bold, friendly, bubbly. Big and short. "Life's short. Jump higher." |
| Subtitle | Cocogoose Pro | Darkmode, ALL CAPS | Small caps tags like "BORN TO MOVE." |
| Body | Cocogoose Pro | Semilight | Paragraphs and labels. |

Cocogoose Pro is a licensed font. If the image tool cannot load it: Fredoka Bold for headlines, Fredoka Regular for body, and note the substitution in the brief.

Listing image minimums (masterclass rule, overrides the guide): headline readable at a 150 px thumbnail. On a 2000 px image: headline 120 px or larger, numbers 160 px or larger, sub text 60 px or larger.

Clearspace: use the width of the letter "e" as the minimum margin on every side. Do not stretch the logo to the edge of a panel.
Size on listing images: small, in a corner. The product and the kid are the hero.
The "H" icon is for the orange rounded-square badge, helmets, and small spaces. On listing images use the wordmark; the icon can sit on a price or feature badge.

## Swoosh element

The brand has a hand-drawn swirl ("fun element," page 2): a loose purple or orange loop that trails behind the product or kid like motion. Use one swoosh per image at most, behind the subject, never over the headline or the product. It reads as movement and ties the stack together.

## Photography and lifestyle

Real kids, roughly 5 to 12, mid-motion: jumping, climbing, scooting, arms up. Big smiles, bright daylight, outdoor or clean indoor. Orange or Light Gray backgrounds on studio shots. The guide's hero shot is a kid mid-jump on an orange field with a purple swoosh.
Masterclass additions: no plastic-looking AI kids, no impossible poses, one kid per image unless showing capacity, helmets on when the product is a ride-on, realistic ground and surroundings. Safety claims (weight limit, age range) must come from the listing facts.

## Voice (headlines and sub text)

Short, punchy, two-beat lines. Examples from the guide:
- Life's short. Jump higher.
- Find your fast.
- Wobble now, wow later.
- Move (little) mountains.
- Born to move. Built to zoom.
Pattern: a setup, a period, a payoff. Three to five words. Parenthetical asides allowed. Always paired with a plain-language benefit line underneath in Semilight.

## Default stack layout for a Hurtle listing (locked across siblings)

1. Hero: product on white per Amazon rules. Value prop on the brown box behind it or a Purple label. Color swatches if multi-color.
2. The ONE value prop: big number in White on Orange (weight limit, age range, max height), product large, one swoosh.
3. Safety image: what keeps the kid safe (grip, padding, non-slip, adjustable), shown close up, White on Purple panel.
4. Dimensions and fit in place (fits in the trunk, in the yard, kid of X height on it), Light Gray background.
5. Lifestyle: real kid mid-motion outdoors, two-beat headline on Orange panel.
6. How to set up or adjust, full height per step, Semilight labels.
7. Us vs them or before/after (wobbly cheap one vs ours, or "screen time vs outside").
8. What's in the box only if it earns the slot.

Locked: colors, fonts, logo position, swoosh placement, panel style, order, backgrounds. Adapts per sibling: the numbers, the swatches, the product render, the kid's age range.
