# NutriChef brand kit (for Amazon listing images)

Source: NutriChef Brand Guidelines v2 (full PDF in this folder). Short version an agent reads before briefing or generating any NutriChef image. Where the brand guide and the Sound Around image masterclass rules disagree on text size, the masterclass wins: everything must read on a phone thumbnail. The brand guide controls colors, fonts, logo, and tone. Note: this guide actually agrees with the masterclass on the hero (product fills the frame, realistic shadow, food never bigger than the product).

## Who buys NutriChef

Home cooks who want quality kitchen gear without the price tag. Brand values: sharing, creativity, affordability, pleasure. Mission: make quality products accessible so everyone can express creativity in the kitchen. Tone: everyday language, impactful and concise, with a small humorous twist ("Who's the chef?", "Grill & chill", "Give credit to the chef", "Seriously chef"). Images should make cooking look like fun with friends, not a lab.

## Colors (use exactly these)

| Name | HEX | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| NutriChef Green | #52AE32 | 82, 174, 50 | Primary. Headlines, buttons, callout panels. Freshness and modernity. |
| Lime | #C8D400 | 200, 212, 0 | Backgrounds and secondary elements. |
| Orange | #EA5B0C | 234, 91, 12 | Sparingly. Highlight one element (a price, a number, an arrow). |
| White | #FFFFFF | 255, 255, 255 | Text on dark or green backgrounds. |
| Black | #202020 | 32, 32, 32 | Text on light backgrounds. |

The logo pill is a gradient from Lime to Green (left to right). That gradient is for the logo only, not for panels.

Approved text/background pairs:
- White on Green
- Black on Lime
- Black on White
- White on Black
- Green headline on White
- Orange number on White or Lime (accent only)

Seasonal logo variations exist (orange/pink for Mother's Day, brown for BBQ, blue for summer, see PDF page 10). Use them only for a promo campaign the user names. Default is green.

## Fonts

| Role | Font | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headlines, titles, subtitles | Gotham | Black (headlines), Medium, Extra Light | Headlines are often ALL CAPS and stacked ("BBQ SALE", "GRILL & CHILL"). |
| Body, legends, buttons | Roboto | Black, Regular, Light | |

Gotham is licensed. Roboto is free (Google Fonts). If the image tool cannot load Gotham: Montserrat Black for headlines, Montserrat Medium for subtitles, and note the substitution in the brief.

Listing image minimums (masterclass rule, overrides the guide): headline readable at a 150 px thumbnail. On a 2000 px image: headline 120 px or larger, numbers 160 px or larger, sub text 60 px or larger.

## Logo

File: `assets/nutrichef-logo-green-pill-transparent.png` (white wordmark on the Lime-to-Green gradient pill, TM included, transparent outside the pill, 2980 x 756).

- Primary logo (the green pill) must be used on everything except the physical product. On the product itself: black, white, or silver hot foil versions (PDF page 12).
- Restricted area: a margin equal to the height of the letter "c" on every side. Do not place text or other elements inside it.
- Placement over photos: only on a clean, flat area of the image. The guide shows it rejected when placed over a busy area, rotated, or faded.
- Do not add a drop shadow, outline, rotate, stretch, or fade.
- Size on listing images: small, top or bottom corner. The product is the hero.

## Icons

Thin monoline kitchen icons in black (or white on dark), built on the logo grid (PDF page 15). Pans, knives, blenders, ovens, whisks, scales. Use only when large enough to read on a phone. A row of six tiny icons is a wasted slot.

## Photography and image rules (from the guide, and they match the masterclass)

Hero image (PDF page 18):
- Product occupies the maximum space in the frame and sits in the foreground.
- White background. Decorative elements (a few ingredients, a utensil) are allowed only when they help explain what the product does.
- Shadow must be realistic.
- Wrong: product in the background with food bigger than the product. Wrong: wooden tools at a fake scale.

Lifestyle (page 17): product in its real environment with people in the shot (except close-ups). Natural attitude and natural lighting. Kitchen counters, dinner tables, backyard grills.

Corporate visuals (page 16): the everyday reality of a kitchen. Friends cooking together, families at the table.

Image set (page 26): show the product in its entirety and keep the textures of each material (steel, glass, wood) accurate. Add one or two decorative elements that explain the use.

Masterclass additions: realistic food quantities, food the product can actually make, dishwasher-safe parts on the top rack, hand-wash parts in the sink, no AI-looking food or hands, alternate fried and healthy across the stack.

## Voice (headlines and sub text)

Short, punchy, a wink of humor. All caps for headlines is on brand. Examples from the guide: "Who's the chef?", "Grill & chill", "BBQ thrill", "Bake croissants like a chef". Pair a headline like that with a plain benefit line under it in Roboto.

## Default stack layout for a NutriChef listing (locked across siblings)

1. Hero: product on white, fills the frame, realistic shadow, one or two small real ingredients only if they explain the product. Value prop on the brown box behind it or a Green label. Swatches if multi-color.
2. The ONE value prop: big number in White on Green (capacity, temperature range, wattage, pieces), product large.
3. What you can make: four-panel grid, one real dish per panel with the setting under it (temperature, time), Lime background.
4. Us vs them or before/after on the commoditized claim (non-stick, even heat, no smoke).
5. Dimensions in place: under the cabinet, on the counter next to a coffee maker, in the drawer. White or Lime background.
6. Lifestyle: friends or family cooking together, natural light, ALL CAPS headline in Gotham Black on a Green panel.
7. Cleanup: top rack or sink, shown for real.
8. What's in the box only if it earns the slot (sets and kits: yes).

Locked: colors, fonts, logo position, panel style, order, backgrounds. Adapts per sibling: the numbers, the dishes, the swatches, the product render.
