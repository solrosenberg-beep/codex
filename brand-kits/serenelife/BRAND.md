# SereneLife brand kit (for Amazon listing images)

Source: SereneLife Brand Guidelines (full PDF in this folder). This is the short version an agent reads before briefing or generating any SereneLife image. Where the brand guide and the Sound Around image masterclass rules disagree on text size, the masterclass wins: everything must read on a phone thumbnail. The brand guide controls colors, fonts, logo, pattern, and tone.

## Who buys SereneLife

Affordable lifestyle lovers. Americans who want to treat themselves on a budget: paddleboards, massage, saunas, towel warmers, AC units. The brand sells the dream at a price that keeps you smiling. Secondary audience: wholesale and corporate gifting (hotels, offices). Image headlines should feel like a treat you can afford, not a spec sheet.

## Colors (use exactly these)

| Name | HEX | RGB | PMS | Use on listing images |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Teal | #018181 | 1, 129, 129 | 7717 C | Primary. Headline panels, big numbers, callout labels. |
| Lake Blue | #66B2B2 | 102, 178, 178 | 7472 C | Logo color. Secondary panels, accents. |
| Mint | #C7E2E2 | 199, 226, 226 | 628 C at 75% | Soft background for product and dimension images. |
| Stone | #D1CCC9 | 209, 204, 201 | 406 C | Neutral background, dividers. |
| Sand | #EAE8E5 | 234, 232, 229 | Warm Gray 1 C at 50% | Off-white background instead of stark white. |
| Black | #000000 | 0, 0, 0 | Black 6 C | Body text, icons on light backgrounds. |
| White | #FFFFFF | 255, 255, 255 | | Text on Teal, icons on dark backgrounds. |

Palette rule: layer soft tones (Teal next to Lake Blue next to Mint) for a calm, layered feel. Do not pair colors that are close in contrast (Black on Teal, Mint on Sand). Approved text/background pairs:
- White on Teal
- White on Lake Blue
- Black on Mint
- Black on Sand
- Teal on Mint
- Teal on Sand
- Teal on White

Never: Black text on Teal, Teal text on Lake Blue, Mint text on Sand, gradients, off-palette brights.

## Fonts

| Role | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | RT Rondelle | Large, lowercase first word for a casual vibe ("feeling the breeze on my face."). Standard sizes 120, 100, 80, 72, 60, 48, 36, 24 pt. Go big. |
| Subtitle | Roobert | |
| Body | Roobert | Bold for emphasis. Never italic, never underline, never "heavy" weight. Consistent size across one piece. |

Both are licensed fonts. If the image tool cannot load them: Poppins SemiBold for headlines, Inter for body, and note the substitution in the brief.

Listing image minimums (masterclass rule, overrides the guide): headline readable at a 150 px thumbnail. On a 2000 px image: headline 120 px or larger, numbers 160 px or larger, sub text 60 px or larger. Left-aligned or centered, never mixed in one image.

## Logo

Wordmark file: `assets/serenelife-logo-teal-transparent.png` (Lake Blue wordmark, transparent, 1427 x 330).
Icon file: `assets/serenelife-icon-teal-transparent.png` (the "S" icon, supplementary only).

- Default is the full wordmark. The icon is for boxes, business cards, social avatars, not listing images.
- Color: Lake Blue (#66B2B2) on light backgrounds. White on Teal or Lake Blue backgrounds. Black only if color is impossible.
- Clearspace: at least one letter-width of space on every side.
- Size: subtle use is 30% to 50% of the width. On listing images stay at the small end, bottom or top corner. The product is the hero.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor outside the palette, or add effects.

## Pattern

Zen-garden rake lines (concentric curves). Two uses:
- Edge to edge: fills a block with a matching flat color under it, no gaps.
- Overlap: layered over the edge of a photo, always joined to a flat color block of the same tone on one side.
On listing images: a small touch on a callout panel or peeking from one edge. Never over the product, never over the headline.

## Icons and callouts

Flat, thin, monoline, black or white depending on background. No chunky icons, no double outlines, no colored icons, nothing complex.
Callouts: rounded corners, optional pattern touch, circular typography allowed for compact labels. Masterclass rule still applies: an icon that cannot be read on a phone is cut.

## Photography and lifestyle

Calm, serene, light. Water, breeze, warmth, spa, home comfort. American and relaxed. Real people enjoying the moment, product present but not shouting. Soft natural light, uncluttered. Hashtag voice is #livetheserenelife.
Masterclass additions: realistic quantities and setups, no AI-looking people, pets are labs, goldens, dalmatians only.

## Voice (headlines and sub text on images)

- Relaxed: casual, lowercase feel. "towel warmers start at $69.90" not "Towel Warmers Start At $69.90."
- Accessible: price and value up front. "put a better price on happiness."
- Immersive: first person feeling. "feeling the breeze on my face." "warmth spreading through me."
- Never stiff, never "luxuriate in our premium designed-in-Italy products."

## Default stack layout for a SereneLife listing (locked across siblings)

1. Hero: product on white per Amazon rules. Value prop on the brown box behind it or on a Teal label. Color swatches if multi-color.
2. The ONE value prop: big number in White on a Teal panel, product large.
3. Us vs them or before/after on the commoditized claim (Mint background).
4. Dimensions in place (fits in the bathroom, on the balcony, in the trunk), Sand background.
5. Lifestyle: real person, calm setting, lowercase feeling headline in Lake Blue panel.
6. How to use, full height per step, Roobert labels, monoline icons.
7. Cleanup, storage, or care shown in the real spot.
8. What's in the box only if it earns the slot.

Locked: colors, fonts, logo position, panel style, order, background tones, pattern placement. Adapts per sibling: the numbers, the swatches, the product render.
