# Pyle brand kit (for Amazon listing images)

Source: Pyle Brand Identity Guidelines, May 2025 (full PDF in this folder). This file is the short version an agent reads before briefing or generating any Pyle image. Where the brand guide and the Sound Around image masterclass rules disagree on text size, the masterclass wins: everything must be readable on a phone thumbnail. The brand guide controls colors, fonts, logo, and tone.

## Colors (use exactly these)

| Name | HEX | RGB | Use on listing images |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pyle Red | #EF3346 | 239, 51, 70 | Primary. Logo, headline panels, callout labels, accent. Lead with it. |
| Deep Burgundy | #5C191D | 92, 25, 29 | Dark background panels, body text on light backgrounds. |
| Bright Yellow | #FBEF56 | 251, 239, 86 | Headline text on Red or Burgundy. Number callouts (8.8 qt, 900 lb). |
| Light Yellow | #FDFADD | 253, 250, 221 | Soft background for product shots and instructions. |
| Pale Yellow | #FFFFF4 | 255, 255, 244 | Off-white background instead of stark white. Text on dark. |
| Black | #000000 | | Only when color is not possible. |
| White | #FFFFFF | | Logo over photos if Pale Yellow lacks contrast. |

Balance rule: 50 / 40 / 10. Dominant 50%, secondary 40%, accent 10%. Typical listing panel: Deep Burgundy or Red block, Pale Yellow product area, Bright Yellow numbers.

Approved high-contrast text pairs:
- Pyle Red on Deep Burgundy
- Bright Yellow on Deep Burgundy
- White on Deep Burgundy
- Pyle Red on Pale Yellow
- Bright Yellow on Pyle Red
- White on Pyle Red
- Deep Burgundy on Pale Yellow

Never: gradients from brand colors, transparency on brand colors, modified brand colors, vivid non-brand colors mixed in, recolored logo.

## Fonts

| Role | Font | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display headline | Parafina | Medium S | Big, few words. 4 words or fewer when all caps. 80% leading. Tracking 0 to -20 as size grows. |
| Subhead | Manrope | SemiBold | 110% leading. |
| Body / labels | Manrope | Regular or SemiBold | 120% leading. Skip a weight when pairing (Regular + SemiBold, never Regular + Medium). |
| Section labels | Manrope | SemiBold, ALL CAPS, +100 tracking | Small "EXPERIENCE" style tags only. |

Manrope is open source (Google Fonts). Parafina is a licensed display face; if the image tool cannot load it, use Manrope ExtraBold for the headline and note it in the brief.

Listing image minimums (masterclass rule, overrides the guide): headline text must be legible at a 150 px thumbnail. In practice on a 2000 px square image: headline 120 px or larger, numbers 160 px or larger, sub text 60 px or larger. If it does not pass the phone test, make it bigger or cut it.

## Logo

File: `assets/pyle-logo-red-transparent.png` (primary wordmark, Pyle Red, transparent background, 2280 x 900).

- Default: Pyle Red wordmark. On Red backgrounds use Pale Yellow. On Deep Burgundy use Pyle Red. Over photos use White or Pale Yellow. Black and white only when color is not possible.
- Clearspace: keep a margin around the logo at least the height of the "P".
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, add effects, recolor, or crop.
- Stacked and vertical variants exist but are for narrow product surfaces only. Listing images always use the primary wordmark.
- Position on listing images: bottom left or top left corner, small. The product is the hero, never the logo.

## Trust stamp

File: `assets/pyle-trust-stamp-designed-in-usa.png` (full color on light backgrounds).

"Designed in the USA" circle stamp. Allowed on ecommerce images. Use small, in a corner, never bigger than the logo. Versions in the PDF (page 24): full color on light, inverted on Red, white on black, black on white.

## Photography and lifestyle rules

- Warm, vibrant, uncluttered backgrounds or shallow depth of field.
- Recognizably American, everyday settings. Not fancy, not staged, not trying too hard. Feels like home.
- Real people doing what they love. Product featured tastefully in the scene, not floating.
- Leave space for headlines.
- Brand pattern (page 49 to 51 of PDF) can peek in from edges behind a product. Never full bleed over the subject, never over the focal point.
- Masterclass additions: realistic quantities, no AI-looking people or food, pets are labs, goldens, dalmatians, never pit bulls or German shepherds.

## Feature icons

Pyle has a product feature icon set (PDF page 54) that works on light and color backgrounds. Use them only when they are large enough to read on a phone. An icon with a 6-word caption under it is a wasted slot. Prefer one big number plus three words.

## Voice (for headline and sub text on images)

- Clear. Short sentences. No jargon, no fluff, no clichés.
- Conversational. Person to person. Not overly casual, no slang.
- Benefit first, in the buyer's words. "Uses the charger you already own," not "USB-C input."

Taglines you may use as small accents (not as the value prop):
- Turning up your passions. (primary)
- Get into it for less. Get out of it more.
- Get into it. Then get out there.
- Fear less. Fun more.
- Bigger bang. Smaller buck.
- It's that easy!

## Mascot

Max Volume, the megaphone mascot (PDF pages 87 to 89). Stickers, inserts, promo graphics only. Not on Amazon hero or stack images.

## Sub-brands

Pyle Pro (page 92) and Pyle Vintage (page 95) have their own lockups in the PDF. Use the sub-brand lockup when the SKU belongs to that line.

## Default stack layout for a Pyle listing (locked across siblings)

1. Hero: product on white per Amazon rules. Value prop on the brown box behind it or on a Pyle Red label. Color swatches if multi-color.
2. The ONE value prop, big number in Bright Yellow on Deep Burgundy, product large.
3. Us vs them or before/after on the commoditized claim.
4. Dimensions in place (fits under the cabinet, in the trunk), Pale Yellow background.
5. Lifestyle, American home setting, headline in Pyle Red panel, Parafina.
6. How to use, full height per step, Manrope labels.
7. Cleanup or care, shown in the real spot (top rack, sink).
8. What's in the box only if it earns the slot.

Locked: colors, fonts, logo position, panel layout, order, background tones. Adapts per sibling: the numbers, the swatches, the product render.
