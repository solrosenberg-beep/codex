# Squatz brand kit (for Amazon listing images)

Source: Squatz Brand Styleguide (full PDF in this folder). Short version an agent reads before briefing or generating any Squatz image. Where the brand guide and the Sound Around image masterclass rules disagree on text size, the masterclass wins: everything must read on a phone thumbnail. The brand guide controls colors, fonts, logo, pattern, and tone.

## Who buys Squatz

Everyday people who want to get fitter at home without the gym-bro attitude. Tagline: FITNESS UPLIFTED. Mission: "Lifting you to your next level." Values: set the benchmark (nothing basic), innovate (engineers not celebrities), repairable (consumables are cheap generics), healthy fun, flexible (fold, roll, stow), zero judgement. The buyer's questions: will it fit in my apartment, will I actually use it, is it for someone like me. Images should show regular bodies, small spaces, and the fold-away story.

Approved text/background pairs (from the guide):
- Black on Yellow (always, for body copy)
- White on Red (always, for body copy)
- Yellow headline on Red
- Red headline on Yellow (the "reverse color pattern")
- Red or Yellow on Denim
- Red, Denim, or Black on Washed White

Never: any color outside these six, two-color logos, logo strokes, logo on a busy photo area, brand pattern on non-brand colors.

## Fonts

| Role | Font | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Titles | Greycliff CF | Bold | ALL CAPS. "FITNESS UPLIFTED" |
| Headlines | Futura PT | Bold | ALL CAPS always, never lowercase. Apply the color pattern (red and yellow alternating lines) whenever possible. "GAIN MOTIVATION. AND MUSCLE." |
| Body | Greycliff CF | Bold | Black on yellow, white on red. |
| Button | Greycliff CF | Bold | "Learn More" style, red pill. |

Both fonts are licensed. If the image tool cannot load them: Jost Bold for headlines (closest free Futura), Work Sans Bold for titles and body, and note the substitution in the brief.

Listing image minimums (masterclass rule, overrides the guide): headline readable at a 150 px thumbnail. On a 2000 px image: headline 120 px or larger, numbers 160 px or larger, sub text 60 px or larger. ALL CAPS headlines read smaller than they measure, so err larger.

## Logo

Wordmark file: `assets/squatz-wordmark-red-transparent.png` (SQUATZ wordmark, Retro Red, transparent, 871 x 227).
Icon file: `assets/squatz-icon-red-transparent.png` (the squat-figure brandmark, Retro Red, transparent, 448 x 346).

- Wordmark and icon are never shown side by side. The icon is a condensed version for tight spaces or as a design element.
- Colors allowed: Retro Red, Youthful Yellow, Dad Bod Denim, Black, White. Nothing else. On a brand-colored background use only Red, Yellow, Black, or White.
- One color only. No strokes, no outlines, no busy photo behind it.
- On physical products the logo is embossed; on images use the flat wordmark.
- Size on listing images: small, in a corner, on a flat area. The product and the person are the hero.

## Pattern

Stacked repeating "SQUATZ" wordmarks fading from Red to Yellow on a Red background. Allowed on brand colors only. On listing images: one edge or corner at most, never behind the headline or the product. Good for a coupon or "what's in the box" panel background.

## Icons

Thick, filled, heavy icons (not thin line). White on Red or Red on Washed White. Dumbbell, kettlebell, heart, timer, muscle. Only when large enough to read on a phone.

## Photography and imagery

- Grainy, slightly noisy filter for a 60s-70s retro feel. Adjust by eye; not a fixed formula.
- Real people with regular bodies, smiling, in real homes and small spaces. Friends working out together. No 8% body fat posing, no spray tans, no gym-bro.
- Warm, feel-good, a bit cheesy on purpose.
- Products shown folded and stowed as well as in use (the "fold, roll, stow" story).
Masterclass additions: realistic poses the product supports, correct weight and resistance numbers from the listing only, no AI-looking people or hands.

## Voice (headlines and sub text)

"The childhood friend." Good natured, frank, a little ribbing. Always driven, curious, optimistic, warm, inclusive, authentic. Never pushy, reckless, preaching, mushy, patronizing, apologizing.
- On brand: "Challenge you, not the poster body." "Lose the excuse." "Fit. And finish." "Own the journey."
- Off brand: "The entire #SquatzFam sends you hugs" (mushy), "excuses don't stand a chance" (pushy).
Headlines are ALL CAPS, short, often two beats: "GAIN MOTIVATION. AND MUSCLE."

## Default stack layout for a Squatz listing (locked across siblings)

1. Hero: product on white per Amazon rules, fills the frame. Value prop on the brown box behind it or a Retro Red label. Swatches if multi-color.
2. The ONE value prop: big number in Yellow on Red (resistance range, weight capacity, pieces, programs), product large.
3. Fits your space: product folded or stowed next to a couch, closet, or under a bed, with the folded dimension. Washed White background.
4. Us vs them or before/after on the commoditized claim (smooth resistance vs jerky, replaceable parts vs throwaway).
5. Lifestyle: regular person or two friends mid-workout at home, retro grain, ALL CAPS headline in the red-yellow color pattern.
6. How to set up or adjust, full height per step, Greycliff labels, thick icons.
7. What's in the box or the repairable-parts story, pattern on one edge.
8. App, content, or community only if it is a real included feature.

Locked: colors, fonts, caps rule, logo position, grain filter, panel style, order. Adapts per sibling: the numbers, the swatches, the product render, the fold-away dimension.
